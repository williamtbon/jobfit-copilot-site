JobFit Copilot Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026

JobFit Copilot is a browser extension that helps users evaluate job postings against their resume and generate job-search guidance, including fit analysis, resume positioning, networking suggestions, and search planning.

Information We Collect

JobFit Copilot may process the following information when you use the extension:

  • Resume text that you upload
  • Job posting text from pages you choose to analyze
  • Information you manually enter into the extension, such as target roles, locations, industries, companies, or networking contacts
  • Basic product usage information, such as feature usage, request success or failure, and error messages

How We Use Information

We use this information to provide the core functionality of the product, including:

  • Analyzing job fit
  • Suggesting resume positioning
  • Creating networking guidance
  • Generating job-search plans
  • Ranking manually provided networking contacts
  • Improving product reliability during beta testing

Limited Use

JobFit Copilot uses user data only to provide and improve user-facing job-search functionality. We do not sell user data. We do not use user data for advertising. We do not transfer user data except as necessary to provide the service, comply with law, protect users, or operate secure infrastructure.

Data Storage

During beta, uploaded resume text may be stored on our backend so the extension can generate personalized results across multiple job analyses. Product usage metrics may be stored in aggregated or pseudonymous form to understand reliability and product value.

Third-Party Processing

JobFit Copilot uses third-party AI and infrastructure providers to process requests and operate the service. User-provided resume and job posting text may be sent to these providers only as needed to generate requested outputs.

User Control and Deletion

Users may request deletion of their stored beta data by contacting the developer at the email address below.
