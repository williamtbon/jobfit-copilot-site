What it does

Your job-search command center.

JobFit Copilot helps users turn a job posting and resume into practical application guidance.

Job Fit Analysis

Compare a job posting against your uploaded resume and identify strongest matches, gaps, and positioning angles.

Resume Angle

Generate truthful resume-positioning suggestions based on the specific role without inventing experience.

Networking Plan

Create role-specific networking search ideas, connection notes, and follow-up messages.

Search Strategy

Build targeted job-search plans based on either the current job posting or your chosen target roles.

Contact Ranking

Rank manually provided networking contacts by relevance to the role or company.

Built for Early Career Search

Designed for students, interns, and early-career candidates navigating high-volume applications.
